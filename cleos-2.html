Taco Salad in Town

1438 Eastport Rd • Jacksonville Northside

A Jacksonville Classic

For decades, locals have driven out of their way for Cleo’s legendary taco salads. Served in a big takeout container, loaded with chips, seasoned meat, beans, fresh lettuce, tomatoes, onions, shredded cheese, and their homemade ranch dressing.

Open weekdays only for lunch. Worth the trip.

What People Order

Large Taco Salad

The one everyone talks about. Chips, lettuce, meat, beans, tomatoes, onion, cheese, and homemade ranch.

The house specialty

Ingredients: Tortilla chips, lettuce, seasoned ground beef, black beans, tomatoes, onion, shredded cheese, homemade ranch dressing.

Homemade Chicken Salad

Regulars swear by the chicken salad sandwich. Simple, fresh, and made in-house.

Ingredients: Shredded chicken, mayonnaise, celery, onion, seasoning, on soft white bread.

Bacon Cheeseburger

Thick, fresh burgers cooked on the grill. Multiple reviews call out the bacon cheeseburger specifically.

Ingredients: Beef patty, thick-cut bacon, American cheese, lettuce, tomato, bun.

Club & Grilled Cheese

Classic deli sandwiches plus grilled cheese with bacon. Straightforward and well-made.

Ingredients: Texas toast, melted cheese, thick bacon, butter.
